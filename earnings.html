My results aren't typical.

This applies to katiep.me, App in 30 Days, the App Idea Engine, the App Design Engine, and my emails and social media posts.

When I mention what my apps have earned, like Tide making $25K in its first 45 days, those are my personal results. They came after years of building and running a business, and they aren't typical.

Many apps, including many built by people who take courses like mine, make little or no money. What yours earns depends on your idea, the market, your competition, your pricing, how much effort and time you put in, and things nobody controls, like App Store rules and changes.

I don't promise or guarantee that you'll earn any money, or any particular amount, from what you learn from me. The revenue estimates in the App Idea Engine are rough guesses based on public App Store data. They are not predictions of what your app will make.
