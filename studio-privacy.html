The short version

Katie P Studio is a private desktop tool I use to publish videos I film and edit myself to my own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, it has no other users, and it does not sell, share, or advertise with anyone's data.

What the app accesses

When I connect my TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): my TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar, so the app can show which account it is posting to.
  • An access token that lets the app upload videos (video.upload) and post them to my profile (video.publish) when I choose to.
  • Creator settings TikTok returns before a post (such as available privacy options and comment, duet, and stitch settings), so I can choose them for each video.

The app does not read other users' data, messages, followers, or any content beyond what is listed above.

Where information is stored

Access tokens and the video files being posted are stored only on my own computer. Videos are sent directly to TikTok's servers when I publish them. Nothing is stored on any other server.

How information is used

Information from TikTok is used only to authenticate my account and publish my own videos with the captions and settings I choose. It is not used for advertising, analytics resale, or profiling.

Sharing

I do not sell or share information with third parties. Videos and captions are sent to TikTok only when I publish them, and TikTok's handling of them is covered by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept on my computer until they expire or I disconnect the app. You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, which deletes its access immediately.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than me.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update the effective date above.
