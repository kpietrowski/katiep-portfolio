So what actually happens?

Four weeks. One app on the App Store.

  1. Week 1

    Your idea, your price, your foundation

    Pick one problem worth solving, see what competitors charge, set your price, and get your app running on a pretend iPhone on your Mac.

  2. Week 2

    The app and the onboarding that sells it

    Build the one thing your app does, then the onboarding: a few questions that turn into a plan made just for your user.

  3. Week 3

    Getting paid

    Your paywall, free trial and purchases, plus ratings and the settings every app needs.

  4. Week 4

    Live on the App Store

    Test on your own iPhone, make your listing and screenshots, submit, and launch.
