Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

App: Tide (contraction timer and labor companion)

Provider: B Gilmore, LLC ("we," "us," "our")

Contact: katie@katiepietrowski.com

The short version

Tide is built to be private. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your device unless you choose to share it. We don't sell your data — ever. The only time your information leaves your phone is when you (1) turn on a share link, or (2) use voice dictation. Both are explained below.

Information in the app

You provide this information; we don't collect it in the background:

  • Contraction timing — start/stop times and the patterns Tide calculates.
  • Updates — text notes, photos, and videos you add.
  • Birth details — optionally a baby's name, date and time of birth, weight, length, a note, and a photo.
  • Voice recordings — only if you tap the microphone to dictate an update (see "Voice dictation" below).

Tide does not collect your location, contacts, health records from Apple Health, device identifiers for advertising, or usage analytics.

Where your information is stored

  • On your device. By default, all of the above is stored locally on your iPhone (and shared with the Tide widget and Apple Watch app through a private app group on your device). Saved "Birth Stories" also live only on your device.
  • In your photo library. When you tap "Save to Photos," an image or announcement card is saved to your camera roll at your request.
  • Apple. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your device backup (which may include Tide's local data) is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

The share link (only when you turn it on)

If you tap "Share this link," Tide creates a private, randomly-generated link and begins sending the contents you've chosen to share — your contraction timing and the patterns Tide calculates from it (such as how long each contraction lasts and how far apart they are), your updates, photos, videos, and (if you announce it) your birth announcement — to our hosting provider, Supabase, so that people you send the link to can view them in a web page. Until you turn sharing on, this information stays on your device.

  • Who can see it: anyone who has the link. The link is a long random token and is not listed or searchable, but treat it like a house key — anyone you forward it to can view the shared content. Viewers can only look; they cannot post, edit, or message back.
  • Contraction data: your contraction start and stop times and the timing patterns Tide derives from them are shared so viewers can follow your live timer and progress. This is health-related information — please share your link only with people you trust.
  • Photos and videos you share are stored on Supabase and served from unguessable URLs so the web page can display them.
  • Turning it off: when you end the session, start fresh, or stop sharing, the link is deactivated and the page no longer shows your information. The link also automatically expires about 48 hours after your last update.
  • Writes are protected by a secret stored only in your device's Keychain, so only your phone can add to your shared session.

Copies of previously shared data and media may remain stored on Supabase after a link is deactivated. You can email us at katie@katiepietrowski.com to request permanent deletion.

Voice dictation (OpenAI)

If you enable voice dictation and tap the microphone, Tide records short audio and sends it to OpenAI to transcribe it into text. We do not store your audio. OpenAI processes it under their API data policies — audio sent through the API is not used to train OpenAI's models and may be retained by OpenAI for a limited period (up to ~30 days) for abuse monitoring, then deleted. If you never use the microphone, no audio is ever recorded or sent. You can turn this off by not using the feature; microphone access is also controllable in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Tide is unlocked with a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles all payment information; we never see or store your payment details. We receive only Apple's confirmation that the purchase is valid.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of providers ("subprocessors") to run these features. Each receives only what's needed for its function:

Shared data is stored on servers located in the United States.

Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: stays until you delete it in the app or delete/reinstall the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.
  • Individual updates: you can delete any update (and its photo/video) inside the app at any time.
  • Shared data on Supabase: deactivated when you stop sharing/end the session, and auto-expires from view after ~48 hours. To request permanent removal of stored copies, contact us at katie@katiepietrowski.com.

Security

Data sent off your device travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your write secret is stored in the iOS Keychain. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information. Because the share link grants access to anyone who has it, please share it only with people you trust.

Children's privacy

Tide is intended for expectant parents and other adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA/UK under GDPR or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to certain processing. We do not sell or "share" your personal information for advertising. To exercise any right, contact us at katie@katiepietrowski.com. Since most data lives only on your device, you can also exercise many of these rights directly by editing or deleting content in the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll revise the "Effective date" above and, for material changes, provide notice within the app or on this page.

Contact
