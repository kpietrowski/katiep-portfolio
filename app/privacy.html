Privacy policy

Your information, plainly.

This policy covers the App in 30 Days course and challenge, its waitlist, the App Idea Engine and App Design Engine, my emails, and my Instagram messages. It's run by me, Katie Pietrowski. (My apps, like Tide, have their own privacy policies.)

The short version

I collect what I need to send you what you asked for, run the course, and get better at helping you. I don't sell your information, and I don't show you ads.

What I collect

When you join the waitlist or get a free tool: your first name and email.

When you use the App Idea Engine or App Design Engine: the app idea you describe, by typing or by talking. If you use the microphone, your recording is sent to be turned into text and isn't kept by me. I also keep the report or design system the tool makes for you.

When you message me on Instagram: your Instagram username, and the name and email you send me.

When you buy the course: your name, email and what you bought. Your card details go straight to Stripe. I never see or store them.

When you use the course: your course key, a random ID for each computer you use, that computer's name (like "Katie's MacBook"), and a scrambled version of your network address. This keeps your course working on your computers and stops keys from being shared.

Automatically: your network address (IP), briefly, so the free tools can't be overloaded. Those records delete themselves within an hour.

How I use it

To send you your report, design system, course key and the emails you signed up for. To tell you when doors open. To run the course and the community. To read the app ideas people share so I can reply personally and make the course better. And to keep everything secure.

Emails

Joining the waitlist or using a free tool adds you to my email list. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and one click takes you off.

Who helps me run this

These companies handle your information on my behalf, only to provide their service:

  • Kit sends my emails and keeps my email list.
  • OpenAI turns your voice into text and powers the App Idea Engine and App Design Engine. Your idea is sent to them to make your report.
  • Vercel and Upstash host this website and store reports, design systems and course data.
  • Stripe handles payments.
  • ManyChat handles Instagram messages.
  • Slack hosts the course community, if you join it.

The free tools also look up public information from the App Store, like competitors' apps and reviews. None of your personal information is sent to Apple.

Your reports and design systems

Each one has its own private link that's hard to guess. Anyone you share the link with can see it, so share it only with people you trust. I keep them for one year.

In your browser

After you sign up, this website remembers your first name and email in your own browser, so you don't have to type them again on the next tool. It stays on your device. You can clear it with "Not you?" or by clearing your browser data. I don't use advertising cookies or trackers.

How long I keep things

Your email list details stay until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. Reports and design systems are kept for a year. Course purchase records are kept as long as I need them for taxes and accounting.

Your choices

You can ask me what I have about you, ask me to fix it, or ask me to delete it. Email me and I'll take care of it. Some records, like purchases, I may need to keep for legal reasons.

Children

The course and tools are for adults. They aren't meant for anyone under 16, and I don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the date below. If it's a big change, I'll email you.
