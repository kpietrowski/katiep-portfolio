“Bought after” counts recipients who have since purchased. It’s a genuine conversion only for campaigns sent to people who hadn’t bought yet — those are marked attributable. For an audience of everyone, recipients may already have been customers, so the same sum would flatter the campaign; the rate is greyed out there. Devices that uninstall are removed when Apple reports the token dead, so a long-running campaign undercounts rather than over.
