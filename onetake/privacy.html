Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

App: OneTake: Home Movie Maker (iPhone)

Developer: B Gilmore, LLC ("we," "us," "our")

Contact: katiepgilmore@gmail.com

The short version: your videos stay on your iPhone. The only things that leave it are the movies you choose to share with family, and those are deleted when you stop sharing them. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your camera roll

OneTake asks for access to your Photos library so it can find the horizontal videos you've already filmed and put them together into home movies, and so it can save new recordings and finished movies. Your videos are read and edited on your iPhone. We don't upload your library, and we don't see it.

Camera and microphone

OneTake uses your cameras and microphone only to record video when you press record. Recordings are saved to your Photos library on your iPhone.

Your TV

When you play a movie on your TV with AirPlay, it goes straight from your iPhone to the TV over your Wi-Fi network. It doesn't pass through our servers. OneTake asks for local network access only for this.

Reminders

If you turn on movie reminders, they're scheduled on your iPhone. No reminder data is sent to us.

Family Link: movies you choose to share

Family Link is optional and off until you turn it on. You choose which movies to share, one at a time.

What we store when you use it

  • A web copy of each movie you choose to share, and a still picture from it
  • The name you give your family page (for example, "The Rivera Family"), and each shared movie's title and dates
  • Your family password, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so we can't read it
  • A random identifier for your copy of the app, used to check your subscription. It isn't your name, email or Apple ID.

Who can see your movies

Only people who have both your family page's link and its password. The link is a random address that can't be guessed. Changing the password signs out everyone who used the old one.

Family members who watch

They don't need an account or the app. When they enter the password, their browser keeps a cookie so it remembers the page, and it can stay signed in for up to a year. To stop people from guessing passwords, our server briefly keeps a scrambled version of the visitor's internet address and a count of wrong guesses. It's deleted within a day.

How long we keep it

  • When you stop sharing a movie, it's deleted from our servers.
  • When you delete your family page, everything on it is deleted.
  • If your subscription ends, your family page stays up for 30 days and is then deleted with all its movies.

Subscriptions

OneTake offers subscriptions for full access. Apple processes all payments through the App Store, and we never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and the random app identifier described above. We use this only to unlock the app and Family Link.

You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

What we don't collect

No analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, and no tracking of any kind. We don't sell or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Children's privacy

OneTake is made for parents and families. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Movies a parent chooses to share may show their children; those movies can be seen only by people with the family link and password, and are deleted as described above.

Your choices

  • Turn off Family Link sharing for any movie, or delete your family page, at any time in the app. This deletes it from our servers.
  • Change Photos, camera or microphone access at any time in iPhone Settings → OneTake.
  • To ask about or delete any data we hold, email us at katiepgilmore@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and describe the changes here.
